# Cooksweet - AI Recipe & Culinary Assistant

Cooksweet is an AI-powered culinary companion and recipe generator built with React, Vite, TypeScript, Express, and Google Gemini API.

## Features
- **AI Recipe Generator ("What's in your fridge?")**: Enter ingredients you have on hand and receive custom culinary recipes.
- **Home & Explore**: Browse curated meals by category (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Vegan, Desserts) with instant search.
- **Full Recipe Breakdown**: Cook time, difficulty, rating, serving sizes, interactive ingredients checklist, and step-by-step instructions.
- **Interactive Cooking Mode**: Step-by-step guided cooker with progress tracking and voice narration.
- **Saved Favorites**: Bookmark recipes with persistent local storage.
- **Dark Mode Aesthetic**: Custom dark theme with warm amber/orange `#FF8C00` branding.
